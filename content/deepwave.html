DEEPWAVE

Status: Previous Work, 2016-2022. I am still the Chairman of the Board, but have stepped down from day-to-day activities.

In 2016 I inherited the German NGO DEEPWAVE e.V.. working on ocean conservation from my father Dr. Onno Groß. Since then, I have taken part in a double leadership position for the organization, as well as being the CTO.

February 2025

Launched

KLARTEXT Meerespolitik

Alle politischen Themen im Meeresschutz verständlich erklärt

klartext.deepwave.org

an interactive and illustrated primer explaining current affairs in ocean governance - comprehensively and in depth.

I’ve designed and implemented the site, wrote an optimized scroll-video implementation and build a custom CMS for our writers. It’s open source!

While I was at it, I also migrated our 20-year-old WordPress page (whose backups had ballooned to 30 GB …) to Hugo, see deepwave.org

December 2019 - June 2022

Writing and working with the “Meeresoffensive 2020”, a policy paper summarizing the joint demands of German nature conservation and development aid associations for effective ocean conservation. The demands were taken up in the coalition agreement of the new German government

July 2021 | June 2022

Director of the Saving Corals - ICRS Filmfestival, and the DEEPWAVE Filmfestival

May 2020

Raising emergency relief funds for families in Tamil Nadu, India (Project Report)

May 2019

Producer of the short film: ONNO (Password: MeeresAdvokat)

October 2018 - Januar 2019

Lead Web Developer for the relaunch of

Wir geben dem Meer eine Stimme! - Mach mit bei DEEPWAVE e.V.

Mit jedem Quadratmeter Schutzgebiet, Verzicht auf Fisch und Plastik können wir die Ozeane und unseren Planeten bewahren. Die Meeresschutzorganisation DEEPWAVE e.V. ...
