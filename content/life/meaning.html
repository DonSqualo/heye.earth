My thoughts

Less epistemic certainty, but I belief having a meaning is a necessary condition for living, because it is the reason you even want to improve in the first place. And not wanting to improve will kill you (physically and mentally) eventually. I think meaningless is an extremely strong predictor of bad mental health and the first principle cause for a lot of suffering globally.
