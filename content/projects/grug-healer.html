all gone now

pain good

but wound need rest, body hard work, not disturb

grug can help! need lick wound and press hard many directions, actually works, enough always

big brain like put “magic” cream on instead of spit, not know why, spit contains magic cream! but grug use magic cream once when wound no heal, pesky mushroom grow, hide body! so happy big brain spend many, many hours not outside find cream

also grug likes go shaman with needle close big wounds many times, very nice. but not big brain, just very skilled grug

pain + stress = bad

grug likes pain, no need go no pain shaman

but big brain always sad, much stress, many asks of body! so no let body heal, much pain always

also brain make pain important, turn into “suffering”, many stories and fear of no heal. big brain very afraid suffering, most evil: make forget universe is nice!

grug remembers and agree with big brain, but have artist poke with colored stick in shoulder (nice pain) and now reads “no stress”, so not have suffering anymore

grug needs teach other grug no stress, prefer real heal wound, but teach easy, so do first!

grug wants find others with no suffering, figure out why so easy but no one does, maybe find out together

some wounds no heal

grug no worried about holes and inside come out, always heal so far

but grug worried many tiny wounds never heal, feel make stiff, not can and want move

one day so many, grug will stop moving, go poof

very bad

also notice mind same problem, not big whirlpool anymore, like when tiny grug! now all rigid, many thoughts not want go, like ugly city with grid

need heal all those scars

grug not know how

many grug and big brain work on heal, much successful. but never stop body from wants scar, and body wants more and more, very greedy when old, make sure end! so sad, not know why

heard ancient grug of all grugs sit under tree long time once, cure all scars

still end, so miss important ones

grug lost, will find way though

grug wants heal many other grugs

grug hug others much force and hum, they heal

not know how or if real, but maybe make feel safe, no stress, wounds heal

grug wants hug all, but arms soo tiny! grug weak, very sad

maybe turn hug into magic cream? not think works, big brain must have found now

but maybe build robot copy grug hug? or grug teach others? really not know how and still not address body wants scars, so need more play with hug in many ways

also grug not want teach others, maybe teach big brain accidentally, make religion

heal more than body

heal also apply to mind, makes away go stress, no more suffering

this very useful, because big brain has very, very bad trait:

if hurt, wants hurt others!

so much earth filled with inside of grugs because of this

grug sometimes like it too, leach out at helper! but have bad memory, so not last long

big brain instead build stones and crosses and many leaves with words say: “here was hurt, will hurt back!” never forget, like scar but of together

grug sometimes wants just make land with this disappear! less suffering, so good, even if big brain things very evil, to much love scar, thinks memory

also unfair to all live in area, grug not make others not exist, even if suffering

better give land big hug, heal scar

(might crush pray building in hug, not worried)

hopes hug robot will help much
